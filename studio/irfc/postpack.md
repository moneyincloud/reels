Holding IRFC? Don't average down before you see this 🚆 Multibagger or trap?

📈 IPO Jan 2021: ₹26 → top ₹229 (Jul 2024), almost 9x
📉 Today (18 Nov 2026): ₹81.10, about 65% below the top

🚆 What IRFC does: it's the money arm of Indian Railways. It borrows from the market, pays for trains, coaches, wagons and railway projects, and leases them to the Railways for ~30 years at cost plus a margin.
• Book (AUM): ₹4.79 lakh cr (Jun 2026), 92% of it the Railways
• Zero bad loans

💰 Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026):
• Revenue ₹7,866 cr, ▲ 23.5%
• Profit ₹1,958 cr, ▲ 10.2%
• Interim dividend ₹1.15/share

⚠️ The catch:
• Interest costs ▲ 30.2%, faster than revenue
• Govt stake cut from 86.36% to 82.90% via OFS (Feb 2026 at ₹104, Jun 2026 at ₹91), and the price dipped both times

Verdict: neither, for now. Priced like a rocket at the top, but the business runs like a train: safe, steady, slow.
