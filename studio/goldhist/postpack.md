## Instagram caption
Gold in India went from ₹3,452 to ₹1,18,421 for 10 grams. But it crashed many times on the way. Here's why 👇

📈 1991: forex crisis, rupee crashed → gold +24.5% in a year (world price actually fell, $375 → $357)
📉 1992: gold imports opened, smuggling premium vanished → −4.5%
😴 1997–2001: Europe's central banks dumped gold; world gold hit $252 (20 Jul 1999). India: ₹5,071 → ₹4,579
🚀 2002–2012: 2008 crisis + US money printing → ₹4,579 → ₹30,164 (6.6x)
📉 2013–2016: Fed "taper tantrum", world gold −29% in 2013 → India −12% over 3 years
📈 2020: Covid, near-zero rates, stimulus → gold crossed $2,000; India +31.6%
🚀 2022–2026: wars + central banks bought 863 tonnes in 2025 → ₹1,18,421 average (FY 2025-26)
📉 Now: strong dollar + hawkish Fed → MCX gold −13.7% since 9 Nov

The pattern: gold rises on fear, a weak dollar and a falling rupee. It falls when the dollar is strong and rates go up.

Prices: RBI Handbook of Statistics, Table 35 (Mumbai average, financial year). Others: World Gold Council, LBMA, Reuters, PTI, INDmoney (MCX).

Not a buy/sell call. Education only. Past moves don't predict future prices.
