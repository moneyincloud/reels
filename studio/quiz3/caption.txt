Money quiz: 3 money questions most people get wrong. You get 3 seconds each 👇

1️⃣ Money grows 8% a year. Years to double? About 9 (rule of 72: 72 ÷ 8 = 9)
2️⃣ Prices rise 6% a year. After 12 years, ₹100 buys about ₹50 worth
3️⃣ Your money falls 50%. To get back, it needs +100%, not +50%

Real example: the Nifty 50 closed at 22,750 on 27 Oct 2026, about 14% below its Jan 2026 high of 26,373. Getting back needs about +16%.
