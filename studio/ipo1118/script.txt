Never trust the subscription number. This IPO lost almost all its listing gain.
Four mainboard IPOs listed in early November. B L S Polymers, S F C Environmental, M K Sons Fine Jewels, and Bombay Coated and Special Steels.
Most people think the more an IPO is oversubscribed, the more it gains. So let's check the scoreboard. Issue price, listing day, and today.
But wait. The least wanted IPO of the four is now beating the sixty four times one.
B L S Polymers. Two hundred forty one times subscribed. Issue price one fifty, closed day one at two sixty nine, up seventy nine percent. On Tuesday, two seventy five.
S F C Environmental. Only nine and a half times subscribed, yet it hit the upper circuit on day one, up twenty nine percent. Now up thirty one.
Bombay Coated. Sixty four times subscribed, up sixteen percent on day one, then three lower circuits in a row. Now just one point seven percent above issue. And M K Sons? Five times subscribed, flat on day one, now up sixteen percent.
So here's the answer. Subscription shows demand on bidding day, not where the price goes next. Listing day is one chapter, not the whole story.
