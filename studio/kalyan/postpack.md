## Instagram caption
From 1 shop in Thrissur to 546 stores in 7 countries 💍🌍 And ₹427 crore profit in just 3 months. Here's how Kalyan Jewellers makes money 👇

🏪 Stores (as per Q2 FY27 investor update):
• India: 365 Kalyan showrooms + 138 Candere + 1 Akshaya Thanga Maligai
• Middle East: 38 (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman)
• USA: 2 · UK: 2
• Total: 546

💰 Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) vs a year ago:
• Revenue ₹10,323 cr, ▲ 31%
• Profit ₹427 cr, ▲ 64% (includes a one-time ₹73.5 cr land-sale gain)
• India: revenue ₹9,007 cr, profit ₹424 cr
• Middle East: revenue ₹960 cr, profit ₹12 cr

🤝 The growth trick: franchise showrooms, where a partner puts in the money and Kalyan runs the store. About 59% of revenue already comes from them, with about 84 more franchised Kalyan showrooms planned for H2.
