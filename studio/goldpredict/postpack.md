MCX gold fell ₹19,621 in 9 trading days (₹1,43,160 on 9 Nov → ₹1,23,539 on 20 Nov, 2:53 PM).

India imports almost all its gold, so the price starts in the US (Comex), in $ per ounce. Comex trades while India sleeps, so by 9 AM the night's move is already done.

📐 The formula (before 3% GST):
₹ per 10 g ≈ Comex $/oz × ₹ per $ ÷ 3.11 × 1.15 (15% import duty since 13 May 2026)

✅ Real check, 18 Nov 2026: $3,797 × 93.44 ÷ 3.11035 × 1.15 = ₹1,31,178. MCX opened at ₹1,31,624 (0.3% apart).

⚠️ It only shows you the next morning. Nobody knows next month: the Fed, the dollar, wars, central banks and import duty can all change it.

Sources: MCX gold Dec futures (INDmoney); spot gold 18 Nov 9:07 AM (Ascendants); rupee close 18 Nov (PTI); Customs Notification 16/2026.
