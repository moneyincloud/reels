## Cast (original characters; keep names, colours and personalities fixed)
- **King Kanagaraja**: kind, curious; asks the questions the viewer would ask. Gold crown, maroon robe.
- **Elder Prince Vel**: spends at once and wants shortcuts. Red tunic. (Ep 1–2: ate his golden fruit.)
- **Younger Prince Arun**: patient, plants seeds back. Green tunic.
- The king's three friends (new from Ep 3):
  - **Minister Mathi**: wise, slow and calm, with numbers on a palm-leaf scroll. Explains the lesson.
  - **Merchant Selvan**: runs the sweet shop the princes own a share in. Knows business and profits.
  - **General Veeran**: brave but impulsive. Falls for tips, borrows and gambles. Learns the hard way.

## Episode format (60–75 s, 10–12 lines)
Hook line (the mistake) → "Welcome back to the King's Treasury" with a 1-line recap → story conflict →
cliff line ("stay with me…") → the payoff with a REAL number (source + date on screen) → three rules →
"Save this, and follow money in cloud for episode N+1" + the hook line again.
Every story number is an illustration, and every real-market number is sourced. No buy/sell calls.
