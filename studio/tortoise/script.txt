Meet Tortoise and Hare. Both want to retire at fifty five. Tortoise starts at twenty five, with five thousand rupees a month.
Hare laughs. I'm young, I'll start at thirty five. And I'll put in ten thousand a month, double. Most people think Hare wins easily.
But wait. This race doesn't end the way you think.
Fifty five arrives. Tortoise put in eighteen lakh rupees over thirty years. Hare put in twenty four lakh over twenty years.
Now the final count. At ten percent a year, just an illustration, not a promise, Tortoise has one crore fourteen lakh. Hare? Seventy six lakh.
Is ten percent a fantasy? The Nifty fifty started at one thousand in nineteen ninety five. Today, thirtieth October, it closed at twenty three thousand. About ten and a half percent a year, with big crashes along the way.
The lesson? Time beats amount. Ten lost years cost more than double the money can fix. Start small, but start early.
