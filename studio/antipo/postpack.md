The company behind Claude lists on Nasdaq on 19 Nov, after 8 PM India time 🚀🫧 A $1.4 trillion IPO: opportunity or bubble?

📋 The offer:
• Ticker: ANT (Nasdaq)
• 825 million shares, price band $70–$78
• Up to ~$64.4 billion raised at the top price
• Valuation ~$1.26–1.41 trillion (fully diluted)

📈 Growth:
• H1 2026 revenue $16.3B vs $1.2B a year earlier (~14x)

• Compute and infrastructure spending is rising fast
• 7 co-founders keep 50.1% of votes via one Class F share
• ~17x run rate at the top price: priced for huge growth
