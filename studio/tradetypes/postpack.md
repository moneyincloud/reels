Intraday vs swing vs F&O trading, explained with real trades from this week 👇

⚡ Intraday = buy and sell the same day. 11 Nov: Kalyan Jewellers opened ₹630, hit ₹689 within an hour, closed ₹664.
📅 Swing = hold for days or weeks. Kalyan went ₹592 (8 Nov Diwali close) → ₹711 (12 Nov) = +20%. Next morning it opened ₹718 and fell to ₹685 in 15 minutes: that's overnight risk.
📜 F&O = futures & options, traded in fixed lots. 1 Nifty lot = 65 units ≈ ₹16.5 lakh (₹25,350 at 9:52 AM, 16 Nov). A 179-point move = ±₹11,642 per lot.

SEBI's latest study (FY26): nearly 9 in 10 individual F&O traders lost money.
