Never trade like the monkey. Eighty eight percent of F and O traders lost money doing it.
Meet Monkey. Every morning he jumps from branch to branch. Buy, sell, options, again. So many jumps, it feels like hard work.
Under the same tree sits Elephant. He barely moves. Most people think the busy monkey must be winning.
But here's the part nobody tells you. Every single jump has a price, even the winning ones.
SEBI checked every individual trader in futures and options, for the year to March twenty twenty six.
Eighty eight percent lost money. Together they lost ninety one thousand six hundred and eighty five crore rupees. The average loser lost one lakh forty seven thousand.
And the jump fees? About twenty four thousand eight hundred crore rupees in brokerage, taxes and charges. Options alone caused ninety two percent of the losses.
The lesson? More jumps don't mean more money. Every jump costs fees and adds risk. Busy is not the same as profitable.
