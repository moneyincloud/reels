Animal Friday: Monkey jumps branch to branch all day. Elephant barely moves. Who's winning?

SEBI studied every individual futures & options trader for FY26 (Apr 2025 – Mar 2026):
📉 87.7% made a net loss
💸 Total loss: ₹91,685 crore
😬 Average loss per losing trader: ₹1.47 lakh
🧾 Costs (brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST): about ₹24,800 crore, ~₹31,628 per trader
🎯 Options caused 92% of all losses

🧠 Busy is not the same as profitable. Every jump costs fees and adds risk.
