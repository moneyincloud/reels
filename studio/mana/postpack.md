## Instagram caption
Profit up 223%, but Manappuram fell 4.6% on 18 Nov 🪙 Multibagger or trap?

🪙 What it does: gold loans. You pledge your gold jewellery, get cash in minutes, repay with interest and take your gold home.

📈 The boom (Q2 FY27, Jul–Sep 2026):
• Gold loan book ₹60,640 cr, up 92.5% from ₹31,505 cr a year ago
• Total AUM ₹73,303 cr
• Profit ₹701 cr vs ₹217 cr, 3.2x

🤝 Bain Capital invested ₹4,385 cr (at ₹236/share) and now jointly controls the company with the founder family (since April 2026).

⚠️ The catch:
• A year ago, microfinance arm Asirvad lost ₹168 cr; this quarter it made ₹32 cr. Part of the profit jump is a bad year going away.
• Gold loans ride on the gold price. If gold falls, the gold behind every loan is worth less.
• Stock ₹283 (18 Nov), about 26% below its 52-week high of ₹381.55

Verdict: strong business right now, but gold-linked. Watch the gold price and Asirvad.
