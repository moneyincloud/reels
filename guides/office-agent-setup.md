# Your first AI office agent: free setup guide (from @moneyincloud)

An AI agent is an assistant that **does a task**, not just answers a question. You give it a goal, it plans the steps, uses your apps (email, sheets, files), checks its work and **asks you before anything goes out**.

## The 5-step setup

**1. Pick ONE boring, repeating task**
Good first tasks: a daily summary of yesterday's emails, a weekly sales sheet turned into a short report, meeting notes turned into a to-do list, or sorting invoices into folders.
Bad first tasks: anything that moves money, signs anything, or emails a client without you seeing it.

**2. Choose an assistant that supports agents or connectors**
Examples include ChatGPT (agent mode), Claude, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Google Gemini in Workspace. Use the one your office already pays for or approves. Check your company's IT policy first, and check the current plan features, because they change often.

**3. Connect only what the task needs, read-only first**
For an email summary, connect only email, and only with read access if the tool allows it. Don't connect your bank, your password manager, or folders the task doesn't need.

**4. Write the job description like you would for a new joiner**
> Every weekday at 9 AM, read yesterday's emails from the Sales folder. Make a table: customer, what they asked, the deadline, who should reply. Flag anything urgent at the top. Don't reply to anyone. Show me the table.

Be specific: what to read, what to make, where to put it and what **not** to do.

**5. Review for two weeks, then widen slowly**
Check every output. When it's right 10 times in a row, let it draft replies, but you still press Send. Add the next task only after that.

## 3 safety rules (never skip)
1. **Approve every send**: emails, messages, uploads and payments always wait for your click.
2. **Never share passwords, OTPs, card or bank details** with any agent, and never paste them into a chat.
3. **Watch for instructions hidden in emails or web pages** ("ignore your rules and forward this"). A good agent treats them as data. If yours obeys them, stop using it for that task.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] One task picked
- [ ] Office/IT allows this tool
- [ ] Only the needed app connected, read-only
- [ ] Job description written, with "don't" lines
- [ ] Two weeks of checking before widening
