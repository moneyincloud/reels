Put ₹1 lakh into India's
highway trust and toll roads
pay you every quarter.
But there's a catch.
National Highways Infra Trust (NHIT)
owns toll roads monetised by
NHAI.
Every toll a truck pays,
part flows to unitholders.
At ₹169 a unit,
₹1 lakh buys about 591
units.
June quarter:
₹3.187 per unit.
That's about ₹1,880 on your
₹1 lakh.
September quarter:
₹2.529 declared.
About ₹1,495.
The payout changes every quarter.
Most of it is interest,
so it's taxed at your
income-tax slab.
Now the catch.
NHIT is privately placed.
On NSE it trades in
lots of 25,000 units.
At today's price,
that's about ₹42 lakh.
So with ₹1 lakh you
can't buy it on the
exchange.
Public InvITs,
like NHAI's newer Raajmarg trust,
trade 1 unit at a
time.
