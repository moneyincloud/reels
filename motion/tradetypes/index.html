Intraday,
swing or F&O?
What each really means,
with real trades from last
week.
Intraday =
buy and sell the same
day.
11 Nov:
Kalyan Jewellers opened at ₹630
and hit ₹689 within an
hour.
But an intraday trade must
close the same day.
By the closing bell it
slipped back to ₹664.
Swing trading =
hold for days or weeks.
From the Diwali close of
₹592,
Kalyan reached ₹711 in 3
sessions.
That's +20%.
The risk?
You hold overnight.
Next morning it opened at
₹718 and dropped to ₹685
in 15 minutes.
F&O =
futures and options:
contracts on a stock or
index,
traded in fixed lots.
1 Nifty lot =
65 units ≈ ₹16.5 lakh
of Nifty.
This morning Nifty futures rose
179 points.
That's ≈ ₹11,600 on one
lot,
and the same loss if
you bet the other way.
That leverage is why SEBI
found nearly 9 in 10
F&O traders lost money.
So which one do you
do?
