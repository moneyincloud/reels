# ITC: the factory, cut open — treatment (Assembly-Line Diagram)

9:16 Instagram reel for @moneyincloud, ~70 s, English female voice (af_heart). No subtitles (owner rule), so no work tickets.
Data: ITC Q2 FY27 results (quarter to 30 Sep 2026, published 2 Nov 2026); share prices NSE via INDmoney, 23 Nov 2026.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One cargo, top to bottom** — the quarter's sales enter at the roof, get sorted into four coloured streams, each floor is one business, all profit drains to a vault in the basement; great pull-back at the end. *Chosen*: a vertical 9:16 frame is a natural tower; the tilt-down between floors is the style's own "chute" move; pipe thickness can show that cigarettes still carry ~74% of segment profit.
2. Two cargoes racing (cigarette rupee vs FMCG rupee) — strong, but halves the screen and the numbers.
3. The factory at two times (2025 vs 2026) — needs too much text for a 70 s reel.

## Logline and arc
ITC's share fell 38% from its high; open the factory and the reason sits on one floor. Setup: porthole on the falling price → turn: the tax gate on the cigarette press → ending: the new floors are growing, but can they carry the old one?

## Opening and ending (different from the library demo)
Opens on the outside event through the porthole (the price line falling), pushes through the porthole into the roof intake. Ends on the great pull-back of a TALL TOWER with four lit floors and a nameplate on the ground line with the share price.

## Score and sound
March ~120 BPM (bassoon bass, clarinet figure, woodblock); each lit station adds glockenspiel; the band stops dead when the EXCISE gate closes and restarts on release. Foley: belt rattle, press thump, sieve shake, roller hum, crane chain, vault clunk, coins. Voice ~10 dB over music, music ducked.
